☁️ What Happens When Released into the Atmosphere?
Anhydrous ammonia gas reacts exothermically with atmospheric moisture to form dense, opaque white clouds of ammonium hydroxide aerosol. Even though pure NH₃ is lighter than air, the moisture-laden cloud becomes heavier than air, hugging ground depressions and building wakes.

Phase 1 · Optics Hardware
Cooled Optical Gas Imaging (OGI) Thermal Cores
Integration with Mid-Wave Infrared (MWIR 3.2–3.4 µm) cooled InSb sensors (FLIR GF320 grade). Utilizes narrowband spectral resonance filtration at hydrocarbon absorption peaks (C-H stretching at 3.3 µm), enabling direct optical visualization of colorless fugitive methane, VOCs, benzene, and propane invisible to ordinary LWIR or visible cameras.
Core Specs:
  • Bandpass: 3.2 µm to 3.4 µm cooled InSb
  • Thermal Sensitivity: < 15 mK (0.015 °C)
  • Leak Detection Limit: 0.8 g/hr CH₄ at 2 meters
  • EPA OOOOa (Quad-Oa) regulatory compliance
Phase 1 · Edge AI
Embedded Edge AI Acceleration (Jetson / Hailo-8)
Hardware-quantized (INT8) custom YOLOv10-Gas neural networks running on embedded micro-hardware (NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano / Raspberry Pi 5 + Hailo-8 26-TOPS NPU). Delivers ultra-low latency inference (<8 ms per frame) with sub-10W power consumption, allowing completely autonomous edge operation in remote pipeline corridors without cloud or internet dependency.
Core Specs:
  • Model: YOLOv10-Gas INT8 quantized
  • Inference Latency: 7.4 ms @ 640x480 (60 fps)
  • Power Envelope: 7W to 12W total system draw
  • Local offline model updates via encrypted USB
Phase 2 · Autonomous Robotics
Autonomous Drone (UAV) & Quadruped Sniffer Patrols
Deployments on quadruped agile robots (Boston Dynamics Spot / Unitree B2) and ATEX Zone 1 rated autonomous aerial drones. Equipped with 360° pan-tilt OGI thermal cameras, 3D LiDAR SLAM, and Tunable Diode Laser Absorption Spectrometers (TDLAS) for continuous automated inspection of high-elevation flare stacks, piperacks, and tank farms.
Core Specs:
  • Autonomous waypoint navigation & dock recharge
  • TDLAS Laser: PPM-meter path absorption
  • Stair & terrain climbing across refinery decks
  • Real-time telemetry relay via Private 5G / LoRaWAN
Phase 2 · Digital Twin
CFD Digital Twin & 3D Atmospheric Dispersion
Direct coupling with 3D BIM/CAD plant models and ultrasonic 3-axis sonic anemometers. Computes real-time Navier-Stokes Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) plume dispersion taking into account building aerodynamic wakes, equipment congestion, thermal convection currents, and terrain topography for pinpoint worker evacuation routing.
Core Specs:
  • 3D facility mesh import (IFC / Revit / glTF)
  • Real-time weather station integration (Wind speed/dir/temp)
  • Dynamic dynamic escape route arrows on worker wearables
  • Automated toxic footprint boundary alerts
Phase 3 · Plant Automation
SCADA / DCS Emergency Shutdown (ESD) Integration
Direct industrial control system communication via Modbus TCP, EtherNet/IP, and MQTT Sparkplug B. Automatically sends hardwired safety trip signals to Emergency Shutdown Valves (ESDV), actuates deluge water/foam curtains, and triggers HVAC positive-pressure isolation dampers in under 500 milliseconds upon dual-sensor leak verification.
Core Specs:
  • Protocol: Modbus TCP & MQTT Sparkplug B
  • SIL-2 / SIL-3 certified voting logic (2oo3 architecture)
  • Valve trip latency: < 450 ms from trigger
  • Full audit trail & sequence of events (SOE) recorder

Industrial explosion-proof hardware design certified for continuous operation in explosive atmospheres (Zone 0 / Zone 1, Class I Division 1). Features copper-free marine-grade cast aluminum enclosures (Ex d), hermetically sealed sapphire optical windows, and intrinsically safe galvanically isolated optical barriers (Ex ia).
